A Beneos creature is more than a stat block and a pretty token. Every monster ships with what a GM actually needs at the table: how to play it, what makes it dangerous, what to read aloud, and how to make its arrival land. The Creature Codex is where that lives. We offer 700+ creatures, 400+ from the SRD and 300+ original Beneos designs, each with its own detail page.

Get a creature into your world

Creatures come from the cloud. Open the Beneos Cloud window, go to the Creatures browser, and install the one you want. Installing creates an Actor in your sidebar with its clean 2.5D token, ready to drag onto the canvas. From there the creature is yours to run, online or off.

Opening the Codex

The Codex is a dedicated window inside the module, a route-based hub so you can jump between creatures without losing your place. Two ways in:

  • From the Cloud window, open a creature's detail page and step into its full Codex entry.
  • From the creature's actor sheet, where a button takes you straight to its page.

What a creature page gives you

Each page is split into clear sections so you find what you need mid-combat without scrolling through walls of text.

Overview

Quick orientation, led by a read-aloud combat starter: a short, evocative paragraph to read the moment the fight begins. The encounter opens as a moment, not a dice roll.

Tactics

How the creature fights. Its approach, where it wants to be on the field, what it fears, the threats it brings. Skim this before you place the token.

Abilities

Its signature, CR-scaled powers, the things that make it feel distinct instead of a reskin. The Combat Autopilot helps you track these in play.

Lore

The background that lets you answer player questions and seed the creature into your world.

Foreshadowing

Short hints to drop before the encounter to build dread, plus a death prompt for when it falls. See Foreshadowing and Immersion for using these well.

Tools on the page

  • Combat Autopilot: a per-creature helper that keeps the fight on rails. See Combat Autopilot.
  • Printable PDF: a clean single-creature PDF for the table or for prep.

Built for immersion

Beneos creatures are made to feel alive on the canvas. Tokens carry FX like drop shadows and a soft glow, and the clean 2.5D art reads instantly even in a crowded scene. The Codex pairs that presence with the words to match, so you run a monster as a scene rather than a number.
