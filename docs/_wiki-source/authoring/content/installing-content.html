Installing is a click. Pick an asset, confirm, and it is in your world, wired up and ready. Sign in first so the module knows what is yours.

Click Install

In the Cloud window, find the asset and hit Install. A short pre-install dialog checks a few things for you: that your membership covers it, whether you already have it (so you do not duplicate it), and for maps, which quality you want (4K or HD).

What lands in your world

On confirm, the asset downloads and unpacks into your world's beneos_assets folder, then the module hooks it up:

  • Creatures become an Actor in your sidebar, animated token and all.
  • Loot and spells become Items you can drag onto a sheet.

Everything is checked on disk afterwards, so you never get a half-installed actor. If a file is missing, the module says so instead of leaving you guessing.

Updates

When we ship a newer version, its listing shows an Update available badge. Reinstall and the new version replaces the old one in place.
