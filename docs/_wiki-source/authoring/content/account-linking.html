Beneos lives in three places: the Foundry module, your Patreon, and the webshop. One thing ties them together, your email. Get the same address connected everywhere and your content unlocks consistently. Get it wrong and things look locked that you actually own.

One email, everything you own

Entitlements are keyed on email. Three addresses resolve to the same account:

  • Your webshop (Shopify) email.
  • Your Patreon email, which syncs about every 20 minutes.
  • The email you sign in with in Foundry (how to sign in).

Match all three and the module and the webshop know exactly what is yours. In Your Library, both Beneos Patreons then read as connected: Battlemaps (1) and Creatures, Spells & Loot (2).

Manage it in Your Library

Open Your Library on the webshop. The sub-navigation has what you need:

  • Account: your profile and connections. Shopify is detected automatically, Patreon you link yourself, and your signed-in Foundry devices are listed.
  • Membership: your active Patreon tier (1), billing, and a Manage on Patreon link.
  • Orders: your shop purchases, handy for confirming a buy went through.

Locked content you should own?

Nine times out of ten it is a mismatched email.

  1. Check the same email is on Patreon, the webshop and the Foundry module.
  2. A brand-new membership can take a few minutes (Patreon syncs roughly every 20).
  3. Still wrong? See the FAQ or contact us.
