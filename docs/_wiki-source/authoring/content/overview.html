What you actually get

Beneos releases are not single images. They are finished, animated Foundry scenes: video backgrounds, walls, lighting, ambient sound, intros, all linked. One click moves you from an overview map to its regions, sceneries and tactical battlemaps. We do the setup in advance. You drop a release in and play. Animated creatures, items and spells come along too.
