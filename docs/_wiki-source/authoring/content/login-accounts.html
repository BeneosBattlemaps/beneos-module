One Beneos account unlocks everything you own, in Foundry and on the webshop. No password, nothing to fill in, about a minute.

Why you need one

Your account is how Beneos knows what is yours. Patreon membership, shop purchases, free releases: they all attach to your account, and the module reads that list to decide what you can install. No account, nothing unlocks. Even a free one is worth it: there is free content of every kind, free maps, creatures, loot and spells.

Use your Patreon email

This is the one thing to get right. Sign in with the same email address you use on Patreon. When you do, your membership links automatically the moment you first log in, and your content is just there. Different email? See the fix at the bottom of this page.

First login creates your account

There is no separate sign-up. You sign in for the first time from inside Foundry, and the moment you type your email and confirm, your account is created on the spot. That is it. Two places to start it:

  • Module Settings: Foundry settings, find the Beneos module, click Sign in to Beneos Cloud.
  • Cloud window: open the Cloud window and sign in from the Home tab.

The login, step by step

  1. Type your email (the Patreon one).
  2. A small popup to beneos.cloud opens.
  3. Confirm it is you, by email or through Patreon, Discord or Google.
  4. Beneos checks your memberships in the background.
  5. The popup closes. You are in, and the button now reads Disconnect Beneos Account.

No popup? Your browser is blocking it. Allow pop-ups for Foundry and try again.

Used a different email than Patreon?

No problem, and no need to start over. Open Your Library on the webshop, go to your Beneos user settings, and add your Patreon email there. Both addresses then count toward your account, and your membership unlocks. More on this in linking your accounts.

What it unlocks

A free account gets the free content immediately. The two Patreons, Beneos Battlemaps and Beneos Creatures, Spells & Loot, add the rest; what each tier gives is in Patreon tiers and the shop. Locked content still shows in the Cloud with a Join prompt, so you always see what a membership would add. Signed in? Go install something.
