Your Library is your home base on the web. The Cloud window brings content into Foundry; the library is where you review what you own, grab downloads, and manage your account.

Get there

Go to beneos-battlemaps.com, sign in, open Your Library. Everything you own or can access, Patreon or shop, is gathered in one view.

The four sections

A sticky bar follows you as you scroll:

  • Library: browse everything you can access.
  • Orders: your shop purchase history.
  • Account: profile and connections.
  • Membership: your Patreon tier.

The browser

The library groups your content into Animated Battlemaps, Creatures, Spells and Items (Adventures are coming). Each entry carries ownership and tier badges plus a download button, so you see at a glance what is yours and at what level.

Two ways to get content

  • In Foundry: install from the Cloud window. Easiest for most maps and tokens.
  • From the web: download the ZIP from your library. What each tier unlocks is in Patreon tiers and the shop.
