A Beneos map is a video, and video asks more of your machine than a still image does. If a scene stutters, you have a few honest levers. None of them touch quality where it matters.

4K or HD?

Most releases let you pick a resolution when you install: 4K or HD. 4K is sharper on a big high-res display but eats more disk, video memory and GPU. HD is lighter and still looks great at table distance. Not sure? Take HD, you can pull the 4K version later. You choose this in the Cloud window.

Switch to Static View

Every animated map also ships as a still. If your system cannot keep up on a scene, switch that one map to its static version, scenery or battlemap, and the stutter is gone. Nothing to re-download. In the Scene Directory, right-click the scene:

  • Use Static Map, swap to the still image.
  • Use Animated Map, switch back to the video.

This is the biggest single win on a weaker machine. Run static for normal play, flip to animated for the reveal.

What costs you frames

  • The animated background is the big one. It decodes video the whole time.
  • FXMaster effects (weather, particles) stack on top. More particles, more cost. Turn them down.
  • Foreground video tiles (waving trees and the like) are extra video layers wherever they sit.

On a weaker machine

  • Install HD, not 4K.
  • Play static, go animated only for the wow moment.
  • Trim FXMaster on any scene that drags.
  • Close other heavy tabs and apps. Foundry runs in the browser and shares your memory with everything else.
