Beneos is a small studio making tabletop content for Foundry VTT. The aim is not loose art to drop in a folder, it is whole, connected experiences: maps that link to each other, monsters that arrive with their own scene, gear that means something. Everything lives in one world, ours, called Escalia.

What we make

Animated battlemaps, cinematic sceneries, 2.5D creature tokens, items and spells. We build the worlds in Unreal Engine 5 and ship them as video, which keeps one consistent look across every map and lets us do things hand-drawn maps cannot: multiple camera angles, player intro sequences, moving parts inside the scene.

The two Patreons

Beneos Battlemaps covers maps, sceneries, handouts and intros, in two tiers (Tableplay and FoundryVTT). Beneos Creatures, Spells & Loot covers the table side: 3D creatures built to feel substantial, many with an intro that shows the monster off before the first roll, plus matching items and spells with printable cards. Its single All Unit tier unlocks the lot. What each tier gives is in Patreon tiers and the shop.

The module

The Beneos Module is the gateway to all of it. It is free for everyone, and it does more for supporters. Inside it you get the Cloud window to browse and install everything, the Creature Codex, the Origins systems, and a stack of free SRD content rebuilt in Beneos quality with custom cards.
