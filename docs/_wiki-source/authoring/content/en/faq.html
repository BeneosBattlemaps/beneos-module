Getting started

What is Beneos, exactly?

Animated battlemaps and fully prepared game content for tabletop RPGs. The maps are animated video; the creatures, spells and loot are static art that comes ready to play, with lore, automation and printable cards. See the welcome page for the big picture.

What do I need to use it?

A membership (Patreon) or a one-time shop purchase, a free Beneos account on beneos-battlemaps.com linked to it, and, if you play in Foundry, the free Beneos module. That is it. See required modules and setup.

Do I need Foundry VTT?

No. You can play three ways: in Foundry VTT (the deepest, one-click experience), in any other VTT such as Roll20 or Alchemy by using the files, or at the table on a TV with any video player. Foundry is optional.

Do I have to buy Foundry VTT?

Only if you want the Foundry experience. Foundry is a one-time purchase from its makers, not a subscription. If you use a hosting service, you enter your Foundry licence key there. You do not need Foundry for table play or other VTTs.

Is there an up-to-date setup guide?

Yes. Start from the in-module Getting Started tour and the quick start video. If you find an older guide online that looks out of date, follow the current one linked from the Beneos website instead.

I am very new to Foundry. How do I use all this?

For general Foundry questions, the many getting-started videos online are a great start. For anything Beneos-specific, our Discord is the next stop, and the quickstart walks you through your first steps with the module.

Is it really as simple as it looks?

Setup is the only part that takes a moment. In Foundry it is one click, and everything that belongs to a map (walls, lighting, sounds, journals, teleporters) comes with it.

Patreon, tiers and pricing

Are Battlemaps and Creatures two different things?

Yes. Beneos Battlemaps and Beneos Creatures, Spells & Loot are two separate memberships. One does not include the other. See Patreon tiers and the shop.

Which Battlemaps tier should I pick?

Tier 1, Tableplay, gives you the maps as files in 4K and HD for the table or any VTT. Tier 2, FoundryVTT, adds the one-click Foundry integration and is the most popular. The full breakdown is on the tiers page.

Does the Battlemaps tier include the creatures or spells?

No. Creatures, spells and loot are their own membership, the All Unit tier on Beneos Creatures, Spells & Loot.

Why are there more tiers than the Patreon page shows?

Over the years some tiers were replaced and hidden when prices or contents changed. Patrons on an older, hidden tier keep their access; new members pick from the tiers shown today.

Do you ever run discounts?

Yes, from time to time. Watch the Patreon and our announcements.

Can I get a creator or affiliate discount code for my community?

Reach out to the team on Discord to discuss it.

Is Discord access linked to Patreon?

Connect your Patreon in your Discord settings to get your member role. Your content access itself runs through your Beneos account and the Cloud, not through Discord. See account linking.

Your Beneos account and access

Where do I sign up, and where is my account?

Everything runs through beneos-battlemaps.com. Your account, your Patreon connection and your downloads all live in Your Library there. The old beneos.cloud address simply redirects there now, so there is no separate site to remember.

How do I create an account and connect Patreon?

Two ways. Either sign up on beneos-battlemaps.com, or just enter your email in the Beneos module inside Foundry: it creates your account automatically and emails you a login code. Then open your account area on the webshop and connect your Patreon. See login and accounts.

Do I have to use the same email for Beneos and Patreon?

It is easiest if you do, but you do not have to. In your account area you connect your Patreon to your Beneos account, and your Patreon email is stored as a second address. So even if you signed up with a different email, on purpose or by accident, connecting Patreon links everything correctly. See account linking.

Where do I find my login for the Foundry module?

It is your Beneos account, the email you signed up with, not your Patreon password. You sign in with a one-time code sent to that email. You manage the account in Your Library on beneos-battlemaps.com.

I am a patron but my content shows as locked. Why?

Almost always an account-linking issue. In your account area, check that your Patreon is connected and your subscription is active. If you used a different email than on Patreon, make sure the Patreon connection is in place, because that is what ties the two together. A brand-new membership can also take a few minutes, since Patreon syncs roughly every 20 minutes.

I did not get the confirmation or login email.

Check your spam folder, make sure you used the right email, and wait a few minutes. If it still does not arrive, ask on Discord or open a support ticket and we will help.

Downloading and installing

Where do I browse and install content?

Everything lives in the Beneos Cloud window inside Foundry, backed by Your Library. Browse there, then install what you need. Walls, lighting, sounds and journals come with each map. See the Cloud window and installing content.

What is the difference between Import all and Update?

Import all brings content from the Cloud into your world so you can use it. Update refreshes content you already have to the newest version. When a newer version exists, its listing shows an update badge.

Do I still need the Moulinette or Scene Packer modules?

No. The current way is the native Beneos Cloud installer built into the module. Moulinette still works as a legacy path during the transition, but it is no longer required.

Can I install a whole campaign at once?

You install releases individually or in bundles today. A single button to install an entire campaign at once is not available yet, mainly because of the very large data size.

Can I download only HD and skip 4K?

Both 4K and HD come with each release. HD is the lighter option for weaker machines. You pick the resolution per map with the 4K / HD switch when you install. See performance and quality.

How do I download without Foundry?

Every tier has a download area in Your Library on beneos-battlemaps.com, where you can grab the files directly as ZIPs, no Foundry needed.

The Cloud window will not open.

Open the console with F12, click Console, then Errors, and see whether an error appears when you press the button. If so, report it on Discord and we will sort it. The Cloud backend is hosted online, so it stays current and we can ship fixes without a module update. Also make sure you are signed in.

I imported content but I get a black screen or a prominent exclamation mark.

This usually means the download timed out or hit an error, so Foundry cannot locate a file (a map background, handout, or icon). Delete the affected scenes and their handouts from the scene and journal sidebars, then install the content again. If it keeps happening, report it on Discord and we will troubleshoot it with you. See installing content.

The maps

What comes with a battlemap release?

The animated battlemap and its sceneries in 4K and HD, a cinematic intro, ambience audio, the static still images, and handouts. In Foundry, walls, lighting and point-of-interest navigation are already set up. See animated battlemaps.

What file format are the maps?

Animated video: WebM inside Foundry, MP4 for the download files. The still images are WebP and PNG.

What is the difference between 4K and HD?

Resolution and file size. 4K is the highest quality; HD is smaller and lighter on performance. Both ship with every release.

Do the maps include journals or DM notes?

Releases come with handouts and journal content where applicable. The specifics vary by release.

How does navigation between scenes work?

Beneos maps use a point-of-interest teleporter: from an overview or world map you jump into the individual scenes, linked for you after install. See the Point of Interest Teleporter and DM navigation.

Does the Point of Interest Teleporter work with non-Beneos maps too?

Yes, the teleporter is a general tool, not limited to Beneos maps. You can add it to your own scenes.

Can I hide points of interest or markers from my players?

Yes, using Foundry's visibility and note-permission settings for the scene.

Can I request a specific map?

You are welcome to suggest maps on Discord. We cannot promise any specific one, but community input shapes the roadmap.

Creatures, spells and loot

Are the creatures, spells and loot animated?

No. Only maps, sceneries and intros are animated. Creatures, spells and loot are static art, but fully prepared for play.

How often do new creatures, spells and items come out?

A new creature roughly every two weeks, a batch of spells once a month, and magic items once a month.

What do I get with each creature?

A 2.5D token and a top-down token, switchable in one click, with one or more variants, plus a Codex: lore, quest hooks, foreshadowing clues, read-aloud text for its entrance and death, and a tactical guide. There is also a Combat Autopilot that plays the creature's turns for you. See the Creature Codex.

How do I switch between 2.5D and top-down tokens?

Right-click the token on the canvas and choose the other style. You can also set a default per creature in the browser.

Are the creatures, spells and loot only for D&D 5e?

They are designed exclusively for D&D 5e, for the statblocks and automation. You can still use the artwork in other systems. For Pathfinder Second Edition we publish every creature on a default sheet so you can at least use the images; there is no D&D-to-Pathfinder translator yet.

Can I print creatures, spells and loot for my table?

Yes. Every creature, item and spell comes with a printable PDF, available online or opened directly in Foundry from a button in the Creature Codex. On top of that, every spell and item has a printable trading card in a custom Beneos design, so you can hand physical loot and spell cards to your players. The card template sits in each PDF and is also shown as a card right inside Foundry.

Is the SRD content free?

No. The full 5e SRD library of creatures, spells and items is part of the paid Creatures, Spells & Loot membership, alongside the Beneos originals.

What is special about the loot?

Every item has an Origin, a theme. Items of the same Origin share bonuses, and attuning several grants set bonuses up to three. With three you can perform a ritual to absorb their powers. Players can also sense nearby items of an Origin they are attuned to, via the in-game item radar. See items and origins.

Can the module make loot or shops for me?

Yes. The Loot Generator rolls a level-appropriate hoard for a chosen size, and the Shop Generator builds a priced shop from an archetype such as blacksmith, alchemist or black market, using SRD and Beneos items.

Why are some creatures or items shown as unavailable?

They are usually monthly loyalty content (see below) or an account-linking issue. If something listed in the shop shows as unavailable in the Cloud, it can be bought in the shop.

Can I get the older loyalty creatures I missed?

Extra creatures released at the start of a month are given free to that month's active patrons and are not added to the standard backlog. If you were not a patron then, you can buy them in the shop.

On the map

A scenery fills the whole screen and I cannot move. Is that a bug?

No. Sceneries are meant to be cinematic, full-bleed views for a roleplay moment, not tactical maps, so there is nothing to move on. Jump back to the tactical version with the battlemap teleporter on the scene. DM navigation covers the icons.

A journal teleporter sends me to the wrong scene.

The journal icon probably has no target journal assigned, or the same journal is assigned to more than one scene. You can relink it manually, and we would appreciate a quick report on Discord so we can fix it for everyone. See the Point of Interest Teleporter for the step-by-step fix.

My player tokens teleport to a random or incorrect spot.

This points to a teleport area whose destination identifier is wrong. There should be exactly two teleporters sharing the same Teleport Identifier. If there are more, one is chosen at random; if there are none, it does nothing. See Multilevel Tokens, and please report faulty teleporters on Discord.

My creatures and tokens disappear under trees or walls. What is happening?

That is by design. We cut out everything taller than the players and place it as a foreground image on top of the tokens, which gives the map a real sense of depth. These are foreground (overhead) tiles. To edit them, open Tile Controls and click the house icon first. For the full explanation see the foreground tiles deep dive.

I see birds, fog, or rain on some maps. Can I stop them?

Many effects are baked into the video itself and cannot be changed. Some scenes also use the FXMaster module for extra filters and particle effects. To remove those, open the magic wand toolbar on the left and use the small trash can icon to clear all effects on the scene. See the FXMaster deep dive.

Why is my intro not playing?

Intros are loopable and paused by default, because players often load onto a scene at different times. The DM starts the intro for everyone by clicking the start and pause tile at the bottom of the navigation, once all players have arrived. Make sure the Monks Active Tile Trigger module is installed and active. See the Monks Active Tile Trigger deep dive.

The map has lots of sounds. Where do they come from, and how loud are they?

Audio is placed as ambient sounds on the sound layer, which you reach from the left control menu, so you can adjust volume, mute, or remove sounds there. Each player and DM also sets their own volumes: the Playlist slider controls DM music, the Ambient slider controls map sounds and sequences, and the Interface slider controls door sound effects. See ambience and sound.

Using Beneos outside Foundry

What do I get for tabletop play with no VTT?

The animated battlemaps and sceneries in 4K and HD as MP4, all the static images, the ambience tracks, the handouts, and the intro sequences. Play them on a TV with any video player.

Can I use Beneos in other VTTs like Roll20?

Yes. You can use Beneos content in any VTT that supports animated video or image files, such as Roll20, Owlbear Rodeo, Fantasy Grounds or Alchemy RPG. There is no one-click installation there: you upload and prepare the files manually, so the experience is more limited. For the full one-click experience we recommend Foundry VTT.

Campaign content and compatibility

Do you offer Curse of Strahd or Descent into Avernus?

We make content that is compatible with popular published campaigns. Curse of Strahd is largely complete, including some homebrew locations, and our current focus is content compatible with Descent into Avernus.

I imported scenes from a published adventure. Where is the written adventure text?

Due to copyright we cannot include the written adventures from Wizards of the Coast. If you own the adventure on D&D Beyond, you can legally import its text into your world using a module like D&D Beyond Importer.

Is more campaign-compatible content coming?

Yes. Around every second release is compatible with the campaign we are currently focused on, and the library keeps growing.

What does a "not yet available" tag on a map mean?

It marks a location that belongs to the campaign set but that we have not released yet. It is on the way, and the tag lets you see the gap in the set so you know it is coming.

Can I use the maps with a reworked or third-party version of a campaign?

Yes. The maps are not tied to a specific ruleset and work with reworked or homebrew versions.

Free content and trying it out

Is anything free?

Yes. Every battlemap release has its static images, watermarked, free to download with a free Beneos account. On top of that, there is a hand-picked free selection of maps, creatures, spells and loot.

How do I get the free files?

Licensing, streaming and commercial use

Can I use Beneos maps in my stream or YouTube videos, even commercial ones?

Yes, without any issue. As a matter of fairness we ask that you say out loud in the video or stream that the content is from Beneos Battlemaps and that there is more on Patreon, with a link in the description. We love links to your games so we can share them on social media. For streams expecting 800 or more viewers, or videos expecting 100,000 or more views, please talk to us beforehand.

Can I use Beneos to advertise a paid game I run?

Public use with the credit above is welcome. For specific commercial arrangements, ask the team on Discord.

Can I redistribute Beneos content or offer it for download?

No, that is not permitted without a separate agreement. If you have an idea or want to use our content in a way that is not covered here, get in touch and we will talk it through. For the full terms see the license page.

Versions, billing and cancellation

Which Foundry and system versions do I need?

Foundry V14, with V13 still supported, the dnd5e system, and the latest Beneos module. See required modules and setup.

Should I upgrade to Foundry V14?

Only if you need a V14 feature. For Beneos content, V14 is fully supported but no better than V13, so there is no rush.

Are the battlemaps tied to a game system?

No, the maps are system-agnostic and work in any system. Only the creatures, spells and loot are built specifically for D&D 5e, although their artwork can still be used in other systems.

Do I keep my content if I cancel?

Anything already installed in your world stays and is not deleted. You just cannot download new files once the membership ends. Anything bought in the shop stays downloadable forever.

Where do I get help?

The Beneos Discord is the best place. Search the FAQ and troubleshooting channels first, and if that does not solve it, open a ticket in the support channel and the team will help. The invite link is on the bug report and contact page.
