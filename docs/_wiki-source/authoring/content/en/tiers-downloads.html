Beneos runs on two Patreons and a shop. Which one you want depends on one thing: how you actually play. Here is the whole picture, short.

Beneos Battlemaps: two tiers, two ways to play

This Patreon has two separate tiers. They are not stacked. Pick the one that matches your table.

TierYou getPick this if
Tier 1, TableplayThe map as a high-resolution video (MP4)You play at a physical table, or in a VTT that is not Foundry
Tier 2, FoundryVTTThe finished, animated Foundry scene, one click into your worldYou run your games in Foundry VTT

Run Foundry? Tier 2 is the one. The whole scene arrives built: walls, lighting, sound, teleporters, all of it. Tier 1 hands you the raw map video instead, which is what you want on a TV at the table or in another VTT.

Beneos Creatures, Spells & Loot: the All Unit tier

One Patreon, one tier. The All Unit tier unlocks everything we make on that side: every creature, every spell, every item. You install them straight into Foundry from the Cloud window, and they come with their codex pages, origins and the rest.

The Beneos Shop

Want a single release without a membership? The Beneos Shop lets you buy one map or creature and keep it for good. It is still coming online, so treat it as the second door for now, not the main one.

Free

A free Beneos account already gets you free content of every kind: free maps, creatures, loot and spells, plus still images. It costs nothing and it is the easiest way to see whether Beneos fits your table.

In your webshop library, each release offers both downloads side by side: the Tableplay video (1) and the Foundry scene (2).

How you actually get it

Inside Foundry, open the Beneos Cloud window and install what you own. Prefer files in hand? Download the ZIP from your webshop library. Either way, make sure your accounts are linked so your access is recognised.

Download links are tied to your account. Do not share them, they carry your name and your access.
