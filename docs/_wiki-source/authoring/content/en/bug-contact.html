Reporting a bug

A clear report helps us fix things fast for everyone. If you can, include:

  • Which release, scene, or asset is affected.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Any console error (press F12, open Console, click Errors, copy what appears when the problem occurs).
  • A screenshot if it helps.

Broken teleporters and faulty teleport areas are especially worth reporting, since fixing them helps every user of that release. The Discord troubleshooting threads include picture-by-picture walkthroughs for the most common cases.
