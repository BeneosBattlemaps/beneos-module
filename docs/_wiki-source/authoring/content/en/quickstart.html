Four steps

  1. Install and activate the companion modules. Beneos content relies on a handful of free Foundry modules. See Requirements for the full list and one-time setup.
  2. Open the Beneos Cloud window to browse and download maps, tokens, items, and spells. See The Beneos Cloud window and Installing content.
  3. Pick a resolution. Most releases let you choose 4K or HD at install time. Unsure? Read Performance and resolution.
  4. Open a scene and play. Walls, lighting, sound, and teleporters are already set. Learn to move between maps in DM navigation and Player navigation.
