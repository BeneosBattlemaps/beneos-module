Beneos maps lean on a handful of free companion modules. Install and activate all of them before you load a Beneos scene, or teleporters, levels and effects simply will not fire.

The five you need

Monks Active TilesRuns the interactive tiles on our maps: world-map jumps, traps, buttons. Deep dive.
Point of Interest TeleporterThe right-click scene jumps the GM uses to move between overview, scenery and battlemap. Deep dive.
FXMasterPaints weather and atmosphere onto a map: rain, fog, embers, snow. This is how you change a scene's mood. Deep dive.
Multilevel TokensMoves tokens between floors and powers the player-facing navigators. Deep dive.
libWrapperA small library the others build on. No settings, just keep it on.

Turn them on

Open Game Settings, Manage Modules, tick all five, save. If a teleporter icon does nothing, or a map looks flat and silent, a required module is off. That is almost always the cause.

Where the content comes from

You do not need a separate marketplace app. Every Beneos map, creature, spell and item installs straight from the Beneos Cloud window inside Foundry. Set up the five modules here, then open the Cloud and start pulling things in.
