This page covers the health warning, age guidance, and usage and license terms for Beneos content and the Beneos Module. For more about who we are, see the about page.

Health Warning for Users with Epilepsy

When using animated Beneos Battlemaps, please be aware of potential health risks, especially for anyone susceptible to photosensitive epilepsy. Some maps contain elements that could trigger a seizure: for example, lightning from storm animations on certain maps produces strobe-like flashes that may act as a trigger. If you or anyone in your group has a history of epilepsy or seizures, please consult a medical professional before using these animated maps. We also recommend taking regular breaks, playing in a well-lit room, and not playing while fatigued or short on sleep, as that can increase the risk of a seizure.

Age Rating Information

Our content has not been rated by any public rating board. We strive to create content that does not glorify violence or brutality. While our fantasy adventure maps may contain traces of violence or depict grotesque creatures, those elements are never the focal point. In the spirit of a safe and inclusive gaming environment, we self-rate our content as suitable for ages 12 and up. We recommend that parents review the maps themselves, or follow our general age guidance, to decide whether the content is appropriate for their children. Our commitment is to provide immersive fantasy experiences without compromising the well-being and safety of our users.

Using Beneos Assets in Streams and Videos

You may use Beneos assets (battlemaps, tokens, spells, items, and so on) in monetized streams or videos without any issue. As a matter of fairness we ask for a verbal mention or an on-screen credit, and we always appreciate links to your games so we can share them on social media. For streams expecting 800 or more viewers, or videos expecting 100,000 or more views, please talk to us beforehand.
