The Beneos Cloud window is the one place you browse, preview and install everything Beneos: creatures, loot, spells and maps. It is a storefront and an installer in one, and you never leave Foundry. What you install lands straight in your world.

Open it

Click the Beneos icon (1) in Foundry's left toolbar (the vertical strip on the canvas). Leave the window open while you work and drag things out of it as you go.

The tabs

Five tabs (1) run across the top of the window:

  • Home: news, the patch log, and where you sign in.
  • Creatures: 2.5D and top-down tokens, each with its actor and codex page.
  • Maps: the animated, fully built battlemap scenes.
  • Loot: items and gear, with their origins.
  • Spells: ready to drop on a sheet.

Find things: the sidebar

The left rail (1) filters whatever tab you are on. Faction, rarity, creature type, biome, source (Patreon, Free, Shop). For maps it also carries the 4K or HD switch (2), pick HD on a lighter machine (why). Toggle list or grid to taste.

Open and install

Click any result and a drawer slides out (1) with the description, a preview, and Install (2) (full flow in installing content). In a hurry? Drag a map, creature or item straight onto the canvas or the Actors sidebar.

Keep it tidy

  • Update available: a newer version of something you own shows a badge. Reinstall to update, nothing to remove first.
  • Orphan cleanup: a maintenance tool clears lost actors and items so your sidebars stay clean.

Locked content

The window shows the whole catalog, including what your membership does not cover yet. Locked content is marked with a Join prompt, so you always see what a membership would add. To unlock, sign in; to see what each tier gives, read Patreon tiers and the shop.
