"You sense something" is one of the oldest tools in a GM's kit. The Item Radar turns it into a real mechanic. Plant a treasure lead in the world and your players physically feel the pull toward it, in fiction, on their own character sheets. It is GM-only, and it makes item hunting a guided adventure instead of a die roll on a random table.

Push a ping (GM side)

The Item Radar lives in the Beneos Live Game Control panel, on its Item Radar tab. GM-only. To plant a lead, push a ping by choosing three things (1):

  • Origin: which Origin the distant item belongs to. Only players attuned to that Origin sense it.
  • Distance: close, mid, or far, roughly 1, 3, or 5 miles.
  • Direction: an 8-point compass heading.

Managing active pings

Pushed pings appear in an Active Pings list, fully live. As the party travels, adjust the distance and direction on the fly so the lead tracks the world honestly. Each ping carries a timestamp so you see how long it has run, and when players resolve it you end the ping.

The Sense Compass (player side)

Any player attuned to an item of that Origin sees a live Sense Compass on their sheet. It points toward the ping and shows its distance, updating as you adjust behind the screen. End the ping and the compass reads "Contact Lost," a clean in-fiction signal that the trail went cold or the prize is claimed.

Because only attuned players sense it, the radar rewards investment in an Origin: a character committed to a set becomes the party's living dowsing rod for more of it.

Running it at the table

  • Plant a ping far at the start of an arc to give the party a direction without a quest marker.
  • Tighten the distance from far to mid to close as they get warmer, and the compass tells the story for you.
  • End the ping the moment the lead resolves, so "Contact Lost" punctuates the find.

Previewing the compass

Want to show a player the Sense Compass before a real hunt? A demo toggle in settings previews it without an active ping, handy for onboarding a table.
