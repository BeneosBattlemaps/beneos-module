Before you read: none of this is needed to use Beneos Battlemaps. Everything is set up for you and works out of the box. This deep dive is for anyone who wants to add Point of Interest functions to their own maps, or is just curious how it works.

What POI does

Point of Interest Teleporter (PoI), like Multilevel Tokens, is rock-solid: it handles thousands of links without complaint. PoI links maps and scenes for the DM, not the players. It gives you navigation across thousands of Beneos scenes and worlds without losing track. Every scene and release follows the same pattern, and teleporters for the same purpose always use the same icons and names.

We wanted to ship experiences, not just maps. A map feels like part of a larger whole only when it is connected to one. That is the job of Point of Interest Teleporters: they take you to overview scenes, the world map, and onward to another zone, to intro sequences, to sceneries. Any DM navigation beyond plain Foundry runs on PoI.

How POI works

PoI works through journals. Normally a journal stores notes or story text. For PoI, the journal itself is the information. In a scene's Scene Configuration, pick a journal under Journal Entry in the Ambience section. That journal is now linked to the scene and gives PoI what it needs. Every scene (battlemap, scenery, intro) has its own unique journal. That is why the Beneos Battlemaps folder in the journal explorer holds so many journals: they store nothing, they just serve as links for PoI.

Drag one of these journals onto the canvas and right-click it (1) for options. View Scene takes you (not your players) to the journal's scene. Activate Scene forces everyone, you included, onto the target scene. So each PoI teleporter targets exactly one scene. Assign a journal in Scene Configuration and that journal represents that scene. Wherever you place it, right-clicking takes you there.

Make icons visible

No journal icons on your maps? Turn them on. In the Foundry toolbar on the left, click the banner (notes) icon (1), then the pinhead icon labeled Toggle Notes Display (2). All journal icons stay visible.

Destination not installed? Install it on the spot

If a teleporter points at a release you have not installed yet, you do not have to hunt for it. Right-click the icon and choose Install Missing Pack. A dialog shows the release, lets you pick 4K or HD with the download size (1), and installs it straight into your world. When it finishes, right-click the icon again and travel. This is GM-only.

Find what is missing

Running a big imported world and not sure which links are broken? Open Module Settings, Beneos Teleporter, Open Audit Window. It scans every scene and lists each broken or missing teleporter: the source scene, the intended target, the status, and the release you would need to install. Go to Source jumps the canvas to the note so you can fix it.

Troubleshooting

A teleporter that lands on the wrong scene is usually one of three things: no journal assigned to the icon, the journal not assigned to a map, or the same journal assigned to more than one map. Each journal must belong to exactly one scene.
