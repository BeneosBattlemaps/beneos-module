Collecting magic items is satisfying. Collecting items that belong together is better. Beneos set bonuses reward a player for attuning multiple items of the same Origin, turning a pile of loot into a growing, themed kit. The more of one Origin a character commits to, the stronger and more characterful the payoff.

The three tiers

Attuning items of the same Origin unlocks tiered bonuses. Three tiers, by how many you have attuned:

TierAttuned itemsWhat you get
Echo of Origin1A small passive, or a once-per-day feature.
Resonance2A stronger active or passive effect.
Perfect Harmony3A capstone effect, the full expression of the Origin.

The fusion ritual

At Perfect Harmony, a character may perform a fusion ritual that combines the three items into a single artifact. Pure upside: the fused artifact still counts as three items for future stacking, so the player keeps their tier and sheds the inventory and attunement clutter of three separate pieces. A clean reward for committing to an Origin.

The Rarity Modifier

Set effects are not all flat. Many scale with the Rarity Modifier: the sum of your attuned items' rarities. Each rarity has a value:

RarityValue
Common1
Uncommon2
Rare3
Very Rare4
Legendary5

Add up your attuned items' rarities for your Rarity Modifier, capped at 15. Chasing the strongest version of a set effect means wanting not just three items of an Origin, but three powerful ones.

Named versus Generic

Named or generic status changes how an item counts toward your set:

  • Named items count toward Resonance and Perfect Harmony, so they absolutely build your tier.
  • But a named item alone does not grant the solo Echo of Origin. The opening tier comes from a generic item.

So a famous artifact slots beautifully into a built-out set, but it will not bootstrap one by itself.

The Beneos Sets tab

Every character sheet gains a Beneos Sets tab that lays out your progress. Per Origin it shows your attuned count, Rarity Modifier, active bonuses, and progress to the next tier. The at-a-glance answer to "what does my gear do together, and what am I one item away from."
