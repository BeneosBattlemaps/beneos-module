GENERAL FAQs:

Where can I go for help with Beneos Content?

  • Answer: The Beneos Discord (BUG REPORT & CONTACT) is the best place to find help. If you search through the FAQ and troubleshooting channels there, you may find your answer up front, otherwise feel free to post a question and we'll help you out!

I'm very new to Foundry, how do I use all this cool stuff?

  • Answer: For general Foundry questions, we encourage you to look through the vast collection of youtube video guides out there (simply search: How to use Foundry and you will see many great getting-started guides!) After that, try our Discord for further help.

The search engine isn't displayed in the Beneos module.

  • Answer: Check the console with F12. Under "Console", click on "Errors" and see if there's an error when you press the Search Engine button. If so, please report this in our Discord, and we'll address it. The search engine is hosted outside of Foundry to always be up-to-date, allowing us to implement hotfixes without module updates.

I imported scenes from a DnD Adventure (like Curse of Strahd), where is the adventure specific written content?

  • Answer: Due to copyright restrictions, we are not allowed to include the actual written adventures from Wizards of the Coast. You can legally import the content into your world from DnD Beyond if you purchase it there using a mod like "DnD Beyond Importer" from Mr. Primate.

IMPORT FAQ:

I imported scenes with Moulinette, and I either get a black screen or some elements display a prominent exclamation point "!", what happened?.

  • Answer: Likely there was an issue during your Moulinette download, and the import either timed out or had an error. If Foundry can't locate the imported content (map background, handout, or icon), it cannot display it. Fortunately, Moulinette is robust and rarely prone to errors. However, if there are internet connection issues (even with high-speed connections), or server outages, this can occur. First, try deleting the faulty scenes and the associated handouts from the scene and journal sidebars. Then, try downloading the content again via Moulinette. If the issue persists, please report it in our Discord. We will troubleshoot with you to fix it. You'll find a link to our discord under BUG REPORT & CONTACT.

When I launch Moulinette Scenes, I don't see most Beneos Content. Where is it?

  • Answer: Moulinette requires authentication through Patreon. This is accomplished using the 'P' button in the Moulinette controls menu on the left side of the screen. Once you authenticate, you will see all the Beneos Content. Note: Authentication opens a new browser window where you click 'OK'. If you have an Adblocker active, that may interfere with the new window opening.

FEATURE FAQs

My players or I, as the DM, can't move properly on a scene. It seems something is restricting our movement.

  • Answer: It sounds like you might have an issue with the Lock-View mod. This module defines the area players can see. For sceneries, it always displays an immersive full screen to players. There are also some movement restrictions on the maps to enhance the experience, like preventing players from seeing the DM's navigation bar below the map. By default, these restrictions are disabled for the DM. If you, as the DM, are still hindered, you can deactivate the effect for the DM or players in the Lock-View settings. Under Module Settings -> Lock View -> User Configuration, you can uncheck "Enable" for any users/DMs you don't want to be affected. For more Information read the DEEP DIVE: LOCK VIEW.

When I right-click a journal icon (POI Teleporter) to navigate to another scene, I'm taken to a random scene instead of the intended destination.

  • Answer: It seems the affected journal icon on the map doesn't have a target journal defined. Foundry, using the Point of Interest Teleporter module, then sends the player to a seemingly random scene. To fix this, you can manually re-establish the link. Go to the 'journal' layer in the left side control menu. Then double right-click the bad journal icon (POI Teleporter) on the map to access the journal options. At the top, under "Journal Entry", select the "DontTouch-POI-Teleporter-****" journal that's also set in the target scene. To verify this, open the scene the journal icon should point to (Right-click -> Configure). Under "Ambience", you'll see which journal is stored there. Once you've linked the journal icon with the scene's journal, the teleporter should work again after a refresh (F5). Additionally, we'd appreciate it if you could report any broken teleporters in our Discord so we can fix them promptly. If you need further help, there is a thread in our Discord (#map-troubleshooting) that explains the process (with pictures!). For more Information read the DEEP DIVE: POI TELEPORTER.

The map has lots of sounds, where does this come from?

  • Answer: With all the remastered packs, the audio is placed as ambient sounds on the 'sound layer' accessible on the left side control menu. You can change the volume, mute sounds or remove them from there. Older packs imported prior to the remaster often have audio embedded directly in the background file itself. This can be muted using the 'ambience' tab in the audio sidebar or with the addition of a module called 'background volume' available in the Foundry module manifest.

When my players use the teleportation areas, their tokens are teleported to a random or incorrect location.

  • Answer: It sounds like the "destination coordinates" might be incorrect. Select the "Shapes" from the left toolbar and double-click on the teleportation area. Under the "Multilevel" tab, you'll see the "Teleport Identifier" which indicates where the teleporter leads. Ensure there are precisely two teleporters with the same identifier. If there are multiple, one is chosen at random. If none are set, the teleporter won't work at all. Additionally, we'd appreciate it if you could report any faulty MLT teleporters in our Discord so we can fix them promptly. For more Information read the DEEP DIVE: MLT TELEPORTER.

I see birds, fog, or rain effects on some maps. Can I stop them?

  • Answer: Most maps have particle effects directly from the Unreal Engine. These are part of the video and can't be changed. However, some scenes also have filters and particle effects created by the Foundry module FXMaster. To deactivate them for a scene, you can open the small "magic wand" for the FXMaster tools on the left toolbar. Using the small trash can icon at the bottom, you can delete all effects on the scene. For more Information read the DEEP DIVE: FX MASTER.

Why isn't my intro playing?

  • Answer: The intros are designed to be loopable and repeat. Since players often arrive at different times after loading onto a scene, the videos are paused by default. The DM can start the videos once all players have arrived on the scene to ensure everyone sees the same thing. The DM can simply click the Start/Pause tile at the bottom of the navigation to start it for everyone. Ensure the "Monks active Tile trigger" module is installed and activated in your world. For more Information read the DEEP DIVE: MONKS ACTIVE TILE TRIGGER.

My creatures and player tokens sometimes disappear under trees or walls. What's going on?

  • Answer: To enhance immersion for all participants, we offer more than just plain maps. We take the extra effort to manually cut out all objects that are taller than the players in the map view and place them as an image "Foreground". This greatly enhances the immersive experience, giving a sense of space and depth when walking under door frames, being overshadowed by gnarled branches, or having parts of walls protruding over the player. This is achieved using "Foreground Layer Tiles". These are like regular tiles and can therefore be accessed via the "Tile" toolbar on the left side. Unlike standard tiles that lie underneath actor tokens, Overhead Tiles, as the name suggests, are ABOVE the tokens and are often used for roofs. To edit these tiles, you first need to click on the small house icon in the toolbar. Only then can Overhead Tiles be edited like any other tile with a double click. An important note: Multiple Overhead Tiles can overlap simultaneously. For example, if you want a roof to be hidden when a player enters a house, but not the door beams and walls. To access the lower layers, move the top ones aside to be able to click on those underneath. Also, remember that tiles are often locked to prevent accidental movement. To unlock, right-click and then click on the small lock icon on the right side of the tile. We explained it in depth with examples in 2. DM NAVIGATION & CONTROLS.
