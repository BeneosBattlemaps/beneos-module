Before You Read

Here you'll find additional information that is not needed for using Beneos Battlemaps. All the sensible functions and settings have already been done for you in advance, and you can use everything immediately and intuitively. This deep dive is meant for those who want to add PoI functions to their own maps or are just curious about how the module works.


Why Point of Interest Teleporter (PoI)?

PoI, like MLT, is a very reliable module that handles thousands of links and connections without any issues. With PoI, we also link maps and scenes in FoundryVTT, but for the DM, not the players. PoI offers navigation capabilities for the DM, so they never lose track among the thousands of scenes and worlds of Beneos. Each scene and release is always structured in the same pattern, and all Point of Interest Teleporters for the same purpose always have the same icons and naming conventions. This makes it user-friendly and easy to use.

For me, it was always important to offer not just maps but experiences. For a map to feel like part of a larger whole, it needs to be connected to it. That's what Point of Interest Teleporters are for. They take us to overview scenes, the world map, and from there to another zone. They take us to intro sequences or sceneries. Any DM navigation that goes beyond Foundry is realized with PoI.

How Does PoI Work?

PoI works through Journals. Normally, Journals are meant for storing information, like notes or story texts. For PoI, the Journal itself is the information. In the Scene Configuration of a scene, you can select a Journal under "Journal Entry" in the "Ambience" section. This Journal is now firmly linked to that scene and gives the PoI module the information it needs. You'll notice that EVERY single scene, whether it's a Battlemap, Scenery, or Intro, has its own unique Journal set in the Scene Configuration. That's why there are so many Journals in the Beneos Battlemaps folder in the Journal Explorer at the top right. These Journals have no information; they merely serve as links for PoI.

When you drag one of these Journals onto the Canvas (the play area in the middle), you can right-click on it to get a selection of options. VIEW SCENE takes you, the DM, but not your players, to the scene of the Journal you dragged onto the Canvas. This means each PoI Teleporter always targets exactly one scene. ACTIVATE SCENE forces all users and you onto the target scene. So, the short version is, if you assign a Journal in the Scene Configuration, this Journal represents that scene. Wherever this Journal is placed, right-clicking on it will always take you to the scene where this Journal is assigned.

Make Journal Icons Visible On Your Scenes

If you can't see any journal icons on your maps, make sure to make them visible! To do this, go to the FoundryVTT toolbar on the left side, click on the banner icon, and then on the pinhead icon labeled "Toggle Notes Display". This will make all journal icons permanently visible.

Troubleshooting

If you're NOT switching to the correct target scene, it's either because no Journal is assigned to the Journal Icon, the Journal itself is not assigned to a map, or the same Journal has been assigned to multiple maps. It's important that each Journal is only assigned to one scene.
