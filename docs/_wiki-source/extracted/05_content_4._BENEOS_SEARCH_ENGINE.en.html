Once you've activated all modules, you'll find a new button in the "Actors" tab on the top right: named "Search Beneos Database". Click it, and our main bread and butter feature, the Search Engine, opens up. We have so much content - working without a search engine is almost not possible. Searching is self-explanatory.

Our Search Engine displays ALL the Beneos content available. There are various sources of Beneos materials, and not every Dungeon Master needs everything. That's why we've separated Maps, Tokens, Spells, and Items. If you come across assets that catch your eye but aren't available to you, you can find them either through one of our Patreons - Beneos Battlemaps or Beneos Tokens, Spells & Items - or purchase them for a small fee in our Beneos Webshop.

The Search Engine is also accessible online.

This means you can search through all Beneos content WITHOUT being a Patreon or shop customer. It's incredibly handy when you're on the go and want to check what you need for your upcoming session.

  • Just visit beneos-database.com to access the online Search Engine

Access the Search Engine In Foundry VTT

Once you have installed and activated the Beneos Module, you'll find a new button in your Actor Browser named "Search Beneos Database." Click this, and the search window will open.

SEARCH MAPS

  1. Here, you can use tags to search through all the items. The options available include:

    • Biome 1 (like Forest, City, Dungeon, etc.)
    • Biome 2 - There are two biome filters available to combine multiple biomes. For example, you can search for horror maps set in a city, or a temple in a forest.
    • Type (Battlemap or Scenery)
    • Brightness (Bright, Medium, Dark)
    • Adventure
    • Map Size (ranging from Tiny to Huge)

  2. Here you'll find the thumbnail of the Battlemap or Scenery. Hovering your mouse over it shows the scene in higher resolution, helping you decide if the map meets your needs. Each Battlemap has its own Scenery, so every Scenery also has a corresponding Battlemap. That's why both are always displayed as a pair, allowing you to see the map from all perspectives.

  3. The Download button directs you straight to Moulinette and filters out the selected map for a one-click download. The Play button takes you to the YouTube video presenting the entire release, showing you how the scene is set up.

SEARCH TOKENS

Beneos Tokens, Spells, and Items cannot (yet) be installed via Moulinette and must first be installed locally in the "beneos_assets" folder. We've explained how to do this in detail in our easy-to-follow setup video.

  1. Here, you can use various tags for your search. Available options include:

    1. Biomes (like Forest, Desert, Dungeon...),

    2. Token Type (Fiend, Humanoid, Devil, etc.),

    3. Fighting Style (Melee, Debuff, Pack, etc.),

    4. CR,

    5. Movement (Walk, Fly, Burrow, etc.),

    6. Purpose (Boss, Debuff, Tank, etc.)

  2. This button allows you to let the Beneos Module search your beneos_assets folder. All newly added Tokens, Spells, and Items will be added to the search engine and can then be used in Foundry. New assets are only available after a manual import.

  3. Here you can see whether the item you're looking for has been locally installed and found. Only Tokens, Spells, and Items have this display since Battlemaps are downloaded via Moulinette and are always available (provided you are a Beneos Battlemaps Patron).

SEARCH ITEMS

Just like Tokens and Spells, Items cannot be installed via Moulinette and must be manually installed. Follow the Beneos Tokens, Spells & Items installation guide to learn how to do this.

  1. Here, you can use Tags to search through all the Items. Available options include:

    • Item Type (Cloth, Trinket, Melee Weapon, etc.)
    • Rarity (Uncommon to Legendary)
    • Origin (Dwarvenforged, Relic, Vampiric, etc.)
    • Price

  2. This button lets the Beneos Module search your beneos_assets folder. All newly added Tokens, Spells, and Items will be added to the search engine and can then be used in Foundry. New assets are only available after a manual import.

  3. Here you can see whether the item you're looking for has been locally installed and found. Only Tokens, Spells, and Items have this display, as Battlemaps are downloaded via Moulinette and are always available (if you're a Beneos Battlemaps Patron).

  4. Here you can see the gold value we have determined for the item. It's a sensible amount for D&D, but it might not be accurate for other systems. Our main goal is to balance Beneos Items against each other, so a more expensive item is always proportionately more powerful than a cheaper one. This is also beneficial if you have a magical shop and want to offer fair and balanced prices for Beneos Items. No other content creator does this, as the math can get quite complex, but we've solved it.

  5. Each item has a specific Origin, and each Origin has its own symbol. The Origin serves to characterize items. For example, Vampiric or Occult Items will always have something evil or unholy about them, whereas Blessed Items are of a divine nature. If players collect multiple items of the same Origin, they receive additional Set Bonuses, encouraging collecting and searching.

SEARCH SPELLS

Just like Tokens and Items, Spells cannot be installed via Moulinette and must be manually installed. Follow the Beneos Tokens, Spells & Items installation guide to learn how to do this.

  1. Here, you can use Tags to search through all the Items. Available options include:

    • Spell Type (Area of Effect, Melee, Protection, etc.)
    • School (Evocation, Illusion, Necromancy, etc.)
    • Level
    • Casting Time (Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, etc.)
    • Class (Wizard, Sorcerer, Warlock, etc.)

  2. This button lets the Beneos Module search your beneos_assets folder. All newly added Tokens, Spells, and Items will be added to the search engine and can then be used in Foundry. New assets are only available after a manual import.

  3. Here you can see whether the spells you're looking for has been locally installed and found. Only Tokens, Spells, and Items have this display, as Battlemaps are downloaded via Moulinette and are always available (if you're a Beneos Battlemaps Patron).
