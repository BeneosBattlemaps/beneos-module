Beneos Battlemaps

Beneos Battlemaps Content, such as map packs and adventures, are installed by yourself in your world using the zip file. This is super easy and is in no way inferior to the Moulinette download, with the advantage that you can archive the zip file and add it to your story at any time, even without an Internet connection, in the best quality.

Proceed as follows:

Go to "Configure Settings" via "Game Settings" in the top right of the FoundryVTT navigation bar. Under the "Library: Scene Packer" tab, click on "Open Importer Wizard". Foundry will now ask you for the location of the zip file and open your File Explorer. Select the location of your zip file and click on "Submit" and the archive will be unpacked and imported into your world. The zip archive is then no longer needed and can be moved or deleted.
