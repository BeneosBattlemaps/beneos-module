BENEOS BATTLEMAPS

Beneos Battlemaps produces battle maps, sceneries, handouts, and intro sequences using state-of-the-art technology. We utilize Unreal Engine 5 to craft high-quality worlds and provide them in the most accessible form - as video files. This ensures all maps maintain a consistent art style and allows us to leverage the numerous advantages of a 3D world over hand-drawn maps. These benefits include multiple perspectives, engaging intro sequences for players, and dynamic elements within the maps to enhance immersion.

BENEOS TOKENS, SPELLS & ITEMS

Beneos Tokens, Spells, and Items offer everything a DM needs. Every week, we release high-quality 3D creatures to populate your world and terrify your players. While these creatures boast incredible detail, complete with their own intro sequences to showcase every menacing detail of a monster before combat begins, their true strength lies in their design. The thought and creativity poured into each creature ensure they feel substantial, boasting exciting abilities without straying too far from traditional DnD design. Unlike others who often reinvent rules due to a lack of clarity in their ideas, we maintain a balance. Additionally, we offer tailor-made items and spells, which come with stylishly designed cards that can be printed and used at in-person gaming tables. With the Beneos search engine, you can quickly find the perfect monster based on preferred biome or creature playstyle. Items, spells, and battle maps can also be easily browsed.

As mentioned, we aim to be a comprehensive indie publisher.

On our website, www.beneos-battlemaps.com, you'll find special token packs and other products designed to captivate and impress your players.

About the Beneos Module:

The Beneos Module is a comprehensive solution for accessing the entire range of Beneos content. While it enhances the experience for Beneos Tokens and Beneos Battlemaps patrons, it's also available for non-supporters. Key features of the Beneos Module include:

  • Beneos Database: An online-backed search engine to sift through ALL Beneos assets, with quick access (drag and drop) to everything locally installed.
  • Exclusive Spells and Items: Designed specifically for Beneos Tokens, Items, and Spells supporters.
  • Additional Quality of Life Features: Tailored for Beneos Battlemaps patrons.
  • Access to Free Content: The module integrates free Beneos and Dungeons and Dragons 5e SRD content in Beneos quality, complete with custom icons and spell/item cards.
